Deep Cleansing for Body and Mind Renewal

Panchakarma is an ancient Indian system of deep body cleansing, allowing you to restore your natural balance and regain vitality.

‍What is Panchakarma?

‍It is a comprehensive program that includes:

  • Diagnostics:: An experienced Ayurvedic practitioner will determine your individual constitution (dosha) and create a personalized cleansing program.
  • Preparatory stage: A special diet, oil massages and herbal saunas will help prepare the body for the elimination of toxins.
  • Main cleansing procedures: Depending on your constitution, five main cleansing methods (Vamana, Virechana, Nasya, Basti, Raktamokshana) will be applied, aimed at removing toxins from various body systems.
  • Recovery stage: A special diet, herbal intake and lifestyle recommendations will help consolidate the cleansing results and restore strength.

The effect of Panchakarma:

  • Deep cleansing of the body from toxins: Improves the functioning of the digestive, lymphatic, circulatory systems, cleanses the skin and respiratory organs.
  • Immunity boost: The body becomes more resistant to diseases and stress.
  • Restoration of energy balance: You will feel a surge of strength, clarity of mind and peace of mind.
  • Rejuvenation of the body: Improves the condition of the skin, hair, nails, normalizes weight.
  • Disease prevention: Panchakarma helps prevent the development of chronic diseases and maintain health for many years.

Give yourself a journey to health and harmony with Panchakarma!
