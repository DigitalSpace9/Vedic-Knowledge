Tours To India

Discover the Magic of India: Life-Changing Journeys

India is not just a country, it's a whole world, full of colors, aromas, ancient traditions and spiritual practices. We invite you to embark on a journey that will leave an unforgettable mark on your soul.

‍Jaipur: The Pink City of Wonders

  • Immerse yourself in history and culture: Visit the magnificent palaces and forts of Jaipur, discover the art of miniature painting and the crafts of Rajasthan.
  • Vibrant colors and aromas: Wander through the bustling bazaars filled with spices, fabrics and jewelry, taste the local cuisine.
  • Spiritual practices: Visit ancient temples, participate in meditation and yoga, discover the world of Indian philosophy.
Indian Himalayas: Journey to Yourself
  • Majestic mountains and the purest air: Enjoy the beauty of the Himalayan peaks, stroll through the picturesque valleys, breathe in the fresh mountain air.
  • Spiritual centers and ashrams: Visit holy places, meet wise teachers, deepen your spiritual practice.
  • Yoga and meditation: Practice yoga against the backdrop of snow-capped peaks, meditate surrounded by nature, find peace and inner harmony.
Our tours:
  • Small groups: Individual approach and attention to each traveler.
  • Experienced guides: Will reveal to you all the secrets and mysteries of India.
  • Comfortable conditions: Accommodation in cozy hotels and travel by comfortable transport.
